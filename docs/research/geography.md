## EEZ / MPA bounding box

The MPA = the EEZ + territorial seas (MPA Ordinance 2016, s.4). The EEZ was
proclaimed in 1997; its fixed boundary points (agreed southern boundary with
French Polynesia, loxodrome legs) are in the Schedule printed in the cap. 48
revised edition (<https://www.government.pn/s/Cap-48-Pitcairn-Islands-Marine-Protected-Area-2017-Rev-Ed.pdf>):

```
26°34'05"S 133°25'29"W → 25°40'40"S 132°59'32"W → 24°04'08"S 132°41'11"W
→ 22°22'55"S 132°23'23"W → 21°03'05"S 132°08'37"W → 20°45'54"S 131°58'43"W
then a clockwise 200-nautical-mile arc back to the first point.
```

**Approximate overall bbox** (derived: fixed points + ~200 nm ≈ 3.33° radius
from the islands) — mark as approximate in the demo:

```
SW corner ≈ (-28.4, -134.6)   NE corner ≈ (-20.7, -121.2)
```

Cross-check: the 2012 Pristine Seas/OBIS survey dataset uses
SW [-25.5, -131], NE [-23.8, -125] just for sampling sites
(<https://nzobisipt.niwa.co.nz/resource?r=pitcairn>) — much tighter than the EEZ.

## Practical facts for trip planning

- **Currency: New Zealand dollar (NZD)** — MSB fees are quoted in NZD. The
  Pitcairn Islands dollar is legal tender but barely circulates (Wikipedia
  outline footnote).
- **Time zone: UTC−08:00** (Pitcairn standard time). Calling code +64 (New
  Zealand). Postcode PCRN 1ZZ; ISO 3166 `PN`; TLD `.pn`.
- **Access**: passenger/supply ship **MV Silver Supporter**, Mangareva
  (Rikitea wharf) ↔ Pitcairn, ~300 mi, **32-hour crossing**; voyages timed to
  the weekly Tuesday Air Tahiti Tahiti→Mangareva (GMR) flight; ~29 return
  services Apr 2024–Apr 2025 (schedule increased ~25% year on year; 2026/27
  schedule shows roughly 1–2 voyages per week in season).
  Typical pattern: depart Mangareva Tuesday → arrive Pitcairn Thursday →
  depart Sunday → back in Mangareva Tuesday. Sources:
  <https://www.visitpitcairn.pn/shipping-schedule>,
  <https://www.visitpitcairn.pn/booking-a-berth>,
  <https://www.visitpitcairn.pn/the-pitkern-blog/now-accepting-bookings-through-to-april-2025>
- Route summary for a researcher: fly to Tahiti → Tuesday Air Tahiti flight to
  Mangareva → short ferry to Rikitea → Silver Supporter 32 h → Bounty Bay
  landing by longboat. Yachts/expedition vessels anchor off Bounty Bay or
  Tedside as directed by the Mayor (Harbour Master).
- 4WD quad bikes are the island transport norm; boat hire, diving and quad
  rental are "negotiated rate with Pitcairn islander provider" (Fees doc).
- Silver Supporter itself is not available for routine fieldwork — "Use of
  Silver Supporter for field work restricted to HMG funded science expeditions"
  (Fees doc).
- Terrain note: roads are steep/unsealed ("Hill of Difficulty"); a second
  harbour ("Alternative Harbour"/Western Harbour) at Tedside was planned to
  give a landing option when easterly swell closes Bounty Bay
  (<http://paulandruthontherock.blogspot.com/2012/>).
