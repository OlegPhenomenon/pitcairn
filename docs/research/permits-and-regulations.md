There is **no "Environment Conservation Ordinance"** in Pitcairn law — the
alphabetical ordinance list on government.pn contains no such title. The
relevant environmental instruments are the MPA Ordinance 2016, the Marine
Conservation Regulations 2022, the Endangered Species Protection Ordinance
2004, the Biosecurity Ordinance 2024, and wildlife rules in the Local
Government Regulations (amended by MCR 2022 reg. 41).

## Zones

1. **Pitcairn Islands Marine Protected Area (MPA)** — "the area comprising the
   Exclusive Economic Zone and the territorial seas of Pitcairn, Henderson,
   Ducie and Oeno Islands" (MPA Ordinance s.4); ~841,995–842,000 km², designated
   September 2016, "third largest fully protected continuous MPA".
   <https://www.gov.uk/government/news/pitcairn-islands-tiny-pacific-community-publishes-ambitious-five-year-management-plan-for-its-vast-marine-protected-area>
2. **Coastal Conservation Areas (CCAs)** (s.11): the territorial seas (12 nm)
   around all four islands + **within 2 nm of 40 Mile Reef** + a **transit zone**
   between Pitcairn and 40 Mile Reef. CCA principles (s.12): conservation,
   sustenance/sustainable development of the Pitcairn community, sustainable
   resource management. In CCAs an otherwise-prohibited activity is allowed if
   conducted per the Regulations/Fisheries Management Plan (s.13).
3. **Transit zone** (MCR reg. 4 + Schedule 1): between the edge of Pitcairn's
   territorial sea (NW) and 2 nm from the 40 Mile Reef summit (SE), bounded N/S
   by outer tangent lines. 40 Mile Reef = seamount ~75 km SE of Pitcairn, "also
   known as Adam's Seamount" (MCR reg. 3).
4. **Specially Protected Areas** (s.7): the Governor may declare any area a SPA
   by order; **no vessel may enter without express permission** of the Governor
   or designee.
5. ~99.5% (832,738 km²) of the MPA is a full no-take zone: "All harmful
   activities, such as fishing mining and dumping waste, which may threaten
   vulnerable marine ecosystems and endangered species are banned within 99.5%
   of the MPA." (gov.uk MPA-plan announcement above)

## Prohibited vs regulated activities (MPA Ordinance ss. 8–10)

Prohibited in the whole MPA (s.8): fishing; any mining activity; disturbance of
or removal of non-living natural material from seabed/subsoil; dumping of waste
or other matter; vibrations likely to adversely affect marine life (other than
ship propulsion); anything else specified as prohibited in the Regulations —
MCR reg. 15 adds: killing/taking/hunting/capturing/harassing CMS Annex I & II
protected migratory species or any other wild seabird (incl. eggs) unless
authorised in writing by the Marine Environment Committee (reg. 16).

Exemptions (s.9): resident in-transit line fishing; **any activity carried out
under a permit "for the purpose of marine scientific research"** (s.9(3));
activities permitted within CCAs.

Regulated activities (s.10) — allowed only per the Regulations: **diving;
anchoring; discharging ballast water**; others specified in regulations (MCR
reg. 13 makes discharge of any polluting substance — ballast, grey water,
sewage, bilge, food waste — a regulated activity; reg. 14 bans discharge in
CCAs). MCR reg. 12: anchoring is allowed anywhere in the MPA unless directed
otherwise by the Mayor, Deputy Mayor, Immigration Officer, Marine Permit
Officer, enforcement officer or Administrator.

## Permit types (MCR 2022)

| Permit | Reg. | Issued by | Max duration | Key conditions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard fishing permit | 6 | Marine Permit Officer (must grant to lawful residents) | 5 years (reg. 24) | artisanal fishing only; FMP rules |
| Extended fishing permit | 7 | Marine Permit Officer | 12 months | non-artisanal purpose/quantity; vessel specified; log/catch record possible |
| Non-resident fishing permit | 8 | Marine Permit Officer | 3 months | for consumption on Pitcairn; accompanied by lawful resident permit-holder |
| Diving permit | 10 | Marine Permit Officer | 12 months | commercial diving operations only; Committee can cap numbers/impose temporary area bans (reg. 11); recreational diving needs no permit |
| **Marine scientific research permit** | 17 | **Governor** (after consultation with the Island Council; must seek advice of the Marine Environment Committee, reg. 23) | 12 months (reg. 24(c): "any other permit") | see below |
| Special activity permit | 18 | Governor | 12 months | for sustainable development of the Pitcairn community or improved MPA protection/management |
| Protected-marine-life authorisation | 15–16 | Marine Environment Committee (written authorisation) | per authorisation | approved purposes: enhance propagation/survival of species, or traditional/customary practices of residents; CMS obligations considered |

### Marine scientific research permit — the one a demo should model

- Reg. 17(1): authorises the named person to undertake the research activities
  specified; **may include otherwise-prohibited activities**; may be limited to
  times/locations/duration; **may include "a requirement that results of the
  research and data collected be made available to the Pitcairn community"**;
  plus any other reg. 25 conditions.
- Reg. 17(2)–(3): any person may apply to the Governor; the Governor may issue
  only if satisfied of the reg. 19 requirements **after consulting the Island
  Council**.
- Reg. 19(1) requirements: (a) the research/activity is **for a purpose of
  benefit to Pitcairn**; (b) any prohibited/regulated activity included is
  **necessary** to that purpose; (c) carried out to **minimise harm** to the
  marine environment; (d) negative impact **proportionate** to potential
  benefit; (e) consistent with MPA/CCA principles. Reg. 19(2): benefit assessed
  against benefit to the marine environment, benefit to the Pitcairn community,
  and (for protected migratory species) CMS obligations.
- Reg. 21 application content: prescribed form; "describe each proposed
  activity in sufficient detail to enable an informed assessment of the
  proposed nature, extent, timing, location, and effects"; who will undertake
  it; purposes; why any prohibited/regulated activity is necessary; how harm
  will be minimised; Governor may demand further information; application fee.
- Conditions (reg. 25): any conditions "to promote the principles" of the
  MPA/CCA — explicitly may include **regular reporting to ECNRD** or another
  specified body.
- Refusal (reg. 26): mandatory refusal if requirements unmet; discretionary for
  unpaid fee, prior offences/permit breaches, or inconsistency with MPA
  principles.
- Notification (reg. 27): written outcome, conditions applied or reasons for
  refusal. Reg. 28: permit specifies activities, authorised persons, date &
  duration, conditions; MPO keeps a record of all permits. Reg. 29: permit must
  be **carried** during the activity.
- Fees (reg. 31): set by Committee notice, may differ for residents vs others;
  waivable in the public interest/exceptional circumstances; paid into "the
  general revenue of the Islands". **No fee schedule for research permits was
  found online** (see Gaps in README).
- Appeals (regs. 35–36): MPO decisions → review by the Marine Environment
  Committee; Committee decisions → review by the Governor.
- Breaches (regs. 33–34): warning or Committee investigation; Committee may
  warn, require training, add conditions, or suspend/revoke.

## Officers & bodies

- **Marine Permit Officer** (reg. 37): appointed by the Governor *by warrant*,
  after consulting the Council; an officer of the Pitcairn Public Service
  (Governor, Administrator and Deputy Governor are barred); is deemed an
  enforcement officer under the Ordinance. Handles fishing/diving permits.
  In absence/conflict, the Committee may act instead. The 2023–24 MPA Annual
  Review names Charlene Warren and Shawn Christian as the Marine Permitting
  Officers.
- **Marine Environment Committee** (reg. 38): chaired by the Administrator;
  ≥2 regular members who are lawful Pitcairn residents appointed by the
  Governor after consulting Council, plus ≥2 reserve members; decides by simple
  majority. Prescribes application forms/addresses (reg. 22 — published on the
  public notice board and the government website), sets fees, oversees permit
  activity, can recommend new regulated/prohibited activities.
- **ECNRD** — Environmental, Conservation and Natural Resources Division of the
  Government of the Pitcairn Islands (MCR reg. 3); permit reporting and
  monitoring conditions reference it; contact mailbox used for research
  applications is `dmnature@pitcairn.gov.pn`.
- Governor → Island Council consultation is required for special permits.
- MPO reports to the Committee at least every 6 months on permits issued,
  enforcement actions, compliance concerns (reg. 39).

## Samples & species protection

- Taking/killing protected migratory species or wild seabirds needs written
  Committee authorisation (reg. 15–16); the MSB Legal Framework notes that
  research "may [need] … a marine scientific research permit, commercial diving
  permit or authorisation to take a protected migratory species or other wild
  sea bird".
- **Endangered Species Protection Ordinance 2004** (cap. 42;
  <https://www.government.pn/s/Endangered-Species-Protection-Ordinance.pdf>):
  implements CITES — export or import of "protected goods" (CITES Appendix
  I/II/III specimens) requires a **licence issued by the Management Authority**
  (s.3); the Governor appoints the Management Authority (s.4) and Scientific
  Authority (s.5); the Governor may by Order prohibit import/export of articles
  or substances endangering endemic/indigenous species, and prohibit taking,
  damaging or killing specified plants/animals (s.6). → Sample/specimen export
  in a demo should reference a CITES/Management Authority licence.
- Biosecurity Ordinance 2024 (made 1 Mar 2024):
  <https://www.government.pn/s/2024-03-03_Biosecurity_Ordinance_signed_and_dated_with_legal_report-fpka.pdf>
- Nagoya Protocol: flagged in MSB docs for genetic resources — draft
  access/benefit-sharing agreements well before collection.

## UK Overseas Territories biodiversity strategy — Pitcairn item (verbatim)

From the "National priorities for Pitcairn Islands → Strengthen and implement
management and regulatory frameworks" list
(<https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/uk-overseas-territories-biodiversity-strategy/uk-overseas-territories-biodiversity-strategy>,
updated 12 January 2026):

> "establish a research permit system to improve data management including
> collection, analysis, reporting and sharing"

Related Pitcairn item in the same chapter (under "Develop skills and talent"):

> "promote the marine science base and encourage scientific expeditions to
> inform and deliver conservation priorities"

Caution — two other research-permit bullets on the same page belong to **other
territories**, not Pitcairn: "maintain and regularly review the criteria for
research permits to align scientific activities with priorities for
conservation…" is under **British Antarctic Territory**, and "establish
research permits and implement regulation for monitoring species outside of the
Wildlife Protection Ordinance" is under **Ascension Island**.
