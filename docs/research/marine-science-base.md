# Pitcairn Islands Marine Science Base (MSB)

Primary source: <https://www.government.pn/marine-science-base> (all documents
below are linked from that page under `/s/…`).

## Overview

- The MSB opened in **February 2024** (page text); the UK government's
  announcement says the Governor to the Pitcairn Islands, Iona Thomas,
  officially opened it. <https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-government-opens-worlds-most-remote-marine-science-base-on-pitcairn-islands>
- Purpose: support the international science community working in the Pitcairn
  Islands MPA (842,000 km², Platinum-level Blue Park Award; "one of the last
  remaining fully intact marine ecosystems").
- Built by Pitcairn residents; locally managed with support from the UK
  Government's Blue Belt Programme; the University of St Andrews was funded by
  Blue Belt to work with residents on management and governance of the
  laboratory. (gov.uk news above)

## Facilities (verbatim from the page)

"The science base is fully equipped with **four bedrooms, a laboratory, a
conference room, kitchen, a living area and with access to high-speed
internet**."

- "Boating, diving and transportation services are available from the Pitcairn
  community."
- The MPA/base does **not** provide vessels: "Researchers who wish to use other
  vessels for their research will need to make arrangements with members of the
  Pitcairn Island community." Own-vessel users must liaise with the Pitcairn
  Island Mayor (Harbour Master) for anchorage locations.

| Stationary [sic] | 10 |
| Lab Assistant (i.e. MSB Manager) | 10 per hour (optional) |
| **Minimum stay per night (p/p)** | **90** |

Conditions (verbatim summary): guests keep the facility clean and wash own
bed sheets/dishes; cleaner available ≈ NZ$40/day; all food paid separately;
boat hire, quad bike rental and scuba diving services at negotiated rates with
Pitcairn islander providers; use of *Silver Supporter* for field work is
restricted to HMG-funded science expeditions; freight/luggage at own expense
unless HMG-funded.

Financial mechanics per the process doc
(<https://www.government.pn/s/MSB-Research-Applications.pdf>): daily charge plus
per-service charges; advance payment may be partly non-refundable on late
cancellation; a refundable **deposit/bond** may be lodged in a holding account
and retained for unpaid damage; cost estimates must be requested "in good
time".

## Governance & roles

From the Legal Framework doc (<https://www.government.pn/s/Legal-Framework.pdf>):

- **MSB Board** — owns the final accept/reject/amend decision; co-chaired by the
  *Environmental, Conservation & Natural Resources Division (ECNRD) Manager*
  and the *UK Government Administrator to the Pitcairn Islands*; ~6 members
  (community rep, Blue Belt rep, SAP chair, MSB Manager); meets quarterly;
  sets charges; reports annually to the Pitcairn Island Government.
- **MSB Science Advisory Panel (MSB-SAP)** — chaired by an independent
  scientist; up to 6 members + MSB Manager as administrator; reviews
  applications and reports to the Board.
- **MSB Manager** — first point of contact ("Project Leader" liaison), handles
  bookings/logistics. The World Association of Marine Stations profile confirms
  the Manager assists "from initial bookings to providing logistical support
  throughout the researcher's stay."
  <https://worldmarinestations.com/discover-pitcairns-marine-science-base-a-remote-sanctuary-for-research-and-conservation/>

## Review criteria (Annex 3)

<https://www.government.pn/s/Annex-3-Review-Criteria.pdf> — MSB-SAP reviews
against (not constrained to) 11 criteria: (1) research context/new knowledge;
(2) objectives clear & logical; (3) approaches/methods appropriate; (4)
anticipated outputs credible and **of benefit to the Pitcairn Island
community**; (5) linkage to the MPA Management Plan and the Research and
Monitoring Plan; (6) practicalities given on-island resources; (7) budget
sufficiency; (8) timeline adequacy; (9) personnel expertise; (10) health &
safety plan robustness; (11) ancillary documentation (insurance, licences,
permits, ethical approvals). SAP recommends; the Board decides: *accept /
accept subject to stated amendments / reject for specified reasons*.

## Operating rules relevant to a demo

- Health & safety framework: UK HSE rules applied — COSHH (chemicals),
  PUWER (equipment), Ionising Radiation Regulations 2017, Control of
  Artificial Optical Radiation Regulations 2010. A **full H&S plan with risk
  assessments** is required with the application.
- Medical insurance: fully comprehensive, incl. **medical evacuation ≥ US$
  500,000**; general liability ≥ **US$2,000,000** naming MSB as additional
  insured (Annex 1 cl. 6). Nearest hospital "days if not weeks away".
- Drones/UAVs and submersible ROVs: must be agreed in advance at application;
  may require a permit.
- Waste: COSHH-consistent handling; researchers generally must **remove all
  generated waste from the island** (no hazardous-waste disposal on Pitcairn).
- Ethics: UKRI code of practice, Animal Welfare Act 2006, Animal Welfare
  (Sentience) Act 2022, IUCN guidelines, **Nagoya Protocol** (genetic
  resources → access & benefit-sharing; draft ABS agreements "well in advance
  of any collection activity"). Institutional ethical approvals supplied with
  the application.
- Prior Informed Consent from stakeholders may be required before sampling.
- Research results must be furnished to MSB (Annex 1 cl. 8(f)); MSB
  contribution must be acknowledged in publications (cl. 8(g)).

## Annex-numbering inconsistency (worth noting)

The "MSB Research Applications" process doc says the application format is in
"Annex 3" and the review criteria in "Annex 4", but the published annexes are
numbered differently (Annex 2 = application form, Annex 3 = review criteria,
Annex 4 = equipment). The process doc appears to use an earlier numbering
scheme.
