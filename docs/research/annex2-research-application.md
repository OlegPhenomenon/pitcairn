# Annex 2 — Application to undertake research involving the use of the Pitcairn Marine Science Base (MSB)

Source document: `Annex 2. Research Application` (PDF), linked from the official
Government of the Pitcairn Islands "Marine Science Base" page.

- Landing page: <https://www.government.pn/marine-science-base>
- Direct PDF: <https://www.government.pn/s/Annex-2-Research-Application.pdf>
- Companion process note ("MSB Research Applications"): <https://www.government.pn/s/MSB-Research-Applications.pdf>

Text below was extracted verbatim from the PDF via `pdftotext` on 2026-10-07.
(The PDF uses a ligature font, so "fi" ligatures render oddly in raw extraction;
wording below is reconstructed faithfully — e.g. "specified" not "speci ed".)

## Preamble / advance-notice rule (verbatim)

> "All individuals or organisations wishing to undertake research which involves
> the use of the MSB must complete and submit this form **at least six month[s] in
> advance** of any proposed research being undertaken. Consideration of shorter
> application times is possible but subject to prior permission from the
> **MSB Manager**."

Note: the companion process document states the same six-month rule but says
shorter times are "subject to prior permission from the **MSB Board**" — an
internal inconsistency between the two published documents
(<https://www.government.pn/s/MSB-Research-Applications.pdf>).

## Submission (verbatim)

> "All applications must be submitted to the MSB Manager
> (e-mail: **dmnature@pitcairn.gov.pn**) by email with all the necessary
> information (….by the published cut off date…). Incomplete applications will
> not be considered."

The parenthetical "(….by the published cut off date…)" is reproduced as printed —
no cut-off date is filled in on the form itself. Reviews happen at quarterly
MSB Science Advisory Panel meetings (see `marine-science-base.md`).

## Signature / declaration part

**The Annex 2 form contains no signature, declaration, or date block** — it ends
at item 17 + the additional-documentation list + the submission line. The
signature block lives in the *Annex 1 User Agreement*, which is executed after
approval (USER: name/date; MSB Facility/Equipment Representative: name/date; plus
Attachment A listing authorised persons with columns: Name / Relationship to User
(indicate whether faculty, research assistant, graduate student, or undergraduate
student) / Gender / Age). Source: <https://www.government.pn/s/Annex-1-User-Agreement.pdf>

## Downstream workflow (from "MSB Research Applications" process doc)

1. Application emailed to MSB Manager → logged, acknowledged by email, applicant
   told which MSB-SAP meeting will assess it.
2. MSB Manager circulates applications to MSB-SAP ≥ 3 weeks before the meeting;
   members use a "review scoring and comment sheet" per application.
3. MSB-SAP (quarterly meetings; short-notice review possible in exceptional
   circumstances) recommends **accept / accept subject to stated amendments /
   reject for specified reasons**.
4. MSB Board takes the final decision (accept / reject / request amendments) and
   informs the applicant in writing, within **two weeks** of the SAP review.
5. On approval: MSB Manager sends the Research User Contract/Agreement
   (Annex 1); fees per the published fee schedule; advance payment and/or a
   refundable deposit/bond may be requested.

Source: <https://www.government.pn/s/MSB-Research-Applications.pdf>
